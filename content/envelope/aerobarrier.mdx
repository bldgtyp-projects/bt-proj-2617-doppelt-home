Hitting Passive House levels of airtightness in existing buildings is hard. The dedicated air barrier layers described
above will give durable airtightness, and they should be the primary strategy. Some areas will be inaccessible because
of existing conditions, or impractical to expose for sealing. Where the air sealing result is uncertain, keep a backup
plan.

[AeroBarrier](https://aeroseal.com/industries/residential-new-construction/) seals a whole building using an
aerosolized polymer that "finds" leaks and closes them. Consult an AeroBarrier installer early in the build so their
work can be scheduled if it turns out to be needed.
