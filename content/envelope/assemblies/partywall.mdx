Target assembly R-value: **R-9** (U-0.11 Btu/hr-ft<sup>2</sup>-F).

- Primary airtightness layer to be [Pro-Clima Visconn](https://475.supply/products/visconn-blueblack) or sim. liquid
  applied airseal applied to the inside face of the existing party-wall masonry. Air sealing matters more than
  R-value here. A shared masonry wall is one of the largest uncontrolled leakage paths in a row house.
- 2x3 stud wall insulated with formaldehyde-free mineral wool batt
  ([Thermafiber](https://www.owenscorning.com/en-us/insulation/commercial/formaldehyde-free) or sim.).
- The party wall faces a conditioned neighbor for most of its height, so it is not modeled as a full exterior
  assembly. Where it stands above the neighboring roofs, at the terrace and penthouse levels, it is exposed to
  outdoor air and must be insulated as an exterior wall.
- Seal every joist pocket, chase and flue penetration in this wall. These are the leaks a blower-door test finds and
  that nobody can reach after finishes go in.
