Target assembly R-value: **R-20** (U-0.050 Btu/hr-ft<sup>2</sup>-F).

- Primary airtightness layer to be [Pro-Clima Visconn](https://475.supply/products/visconn-blueblack) or sim. liquid
  applied airseal, applied to the inside face of the existing masonry. Carry it continuously across the floor lines
  and around every embedded joist pocket.
- [3-1/2" SmartRock](https://475.supply/products/smartrock) semi-rigid mineral fiber plus
  [Intello Plus](https://475.supply/products/intello-plus) installed inside the existing masonry wall. Mineral fiber
  keeps the assembly vapor-open inward so it can dry to the interior. Foam does not.
- 2x service cavity stud wall inside the insulation layer, insulated with formaldehyde-free mineral wool batt
  ([Thermafiber](https://www.owenscorning.com/en-us/insulation/commercial/formaldehyde-free) or sim.).
- Run all plumbing and electrical in the service cavity. Fewer penetrations through the air barrier means a lower
  blower-door number.
- Existing masonry thickness, backup construction and joist-bearing condition are noted as assumed on the drawing set.
  Confirm them before this assembly is finalized.

<div class="btwr-callout btwr-callout--critical">
  <span>
    Insulating solid masonry on the inside changes how the existing wall dries, and the embedded floor joist ends are
    the condition most often damaged when this is done by rule of thumb. Hygrothermal (WUFI-Pro) analysis of the
    front wall, the rear wall and a party wall, including a check at the joist ends, is scheduled in the envelope
    design phase. Do not increase insulation depth beyond what is shown here unless detailed analysis is executed.
  </span>
</div>
