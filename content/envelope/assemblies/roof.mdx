Target assembly R-value: **R-60** (U-0.017 Btu/hr-ft<sup>2</sup>-F).

- Install continuous insulation over the wood-framed roof structure. Use min. 4" XPS, GPS or polyisocyanurate foam
  board or sim.
- Use adhered insulation where possible, and avoid metal fasteners through the insulation layer.
- Primary air barrier to be [Intello Plus](https://475.supply/products/intello-plus) air and moisture vapor retarder
  installed along the underside of all roof joists. If preferred, substitute A/C plywood. Tape all seams to keep the
  layer air and moisture-vapor tight.
- Use a service cavity / drop ceiling below the air and vapor retarder for all plumbing, electrical and lighting
  fixtures. Recessed fixtures punched through the air barrier are the most common source of leakage at a roof.
- Note: when constructing un-vented insulated rafter assemblies, follow all code requirements for condensation
  control at the roof deck. The New York State reference is
  [Residential Code of NYS section R806.5](https://up.codes/viewer/new_york/irc-2024/chapter/8/roof-ceiling-construction#R806.5);
  confirm the governing New York City Building Code provision with the architect, since this building is filed under
  the commercial provisions.
- If constructing the roof without venting, min. R-20 insulation should be used above and outside the primary
  sheathing layer. R-20 can be achieved by:
  - 5" of EPS foam board
  - 4" of GPS foam board
  - 4" of XPS foam board
  - 4" of polyisocyanurate foam board
- The roof is the cheapest R-value on this building. Going deeper here costs no floor area, which is why the
  recommended package carries R-60 at the roof and stops at R-20 on the walls.
- Coordinate the roof insulation depth with the penthouse PV array mounting and with any mechanical dunnage, so
  neither penetrates the air barrier or compresses the insulation.
