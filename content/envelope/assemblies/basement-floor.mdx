Target assembly R-value: **R-23** (U-0.043 Btu/hr-ft<sup>2</sup>-F).

- Note: all concrete sizing as per the Structural Engineer. Concrete is shown for illustration purposes only, and the
  underpinning sequence governs.
- Target 4" sub-slab insulation ([Neopor Graphite EPS](https://neopor-insulation.com/),
  [HFO XPS](https://www.owenscorning.com/en-us/insulation/commercial/foamular-ngx) or sim.).
- Install foam insulation in 2 layers of 2", offset all board edges min. 12", fill all voids and gaps between boards
  with low-expansion spray foam.
- Sub-slab air and vapor barrier to be installed directly below the concrete and above the insulation. Use
  [15 Mil StegoHome](https://475.supply/products/stegohome-15-mil-vapor-barrier) or similar.
- Tape and seal all penetrations and seams of the sub-slab air and vapor barrier with
  [StegoTape](https://475.supply/products/stego-tape) / [StegoMastic](https://475.supply/products/stego-mastic) or
  similar.
- Carry the sub-slab insulation up the inside face of the new foundation wall to meet the wall insulation. Otherwise
  the slab edge stays a continuous thermal bridge around the full perimeter.
- The lowered slab and the new underpinning are the one place on this project where a fully continuous insulation and
  air-barrier layer is actually achievable. Take advantage of it.
