[Total annual energy consumption](#site-energy) is the key figure when evaluating a building's performance, as shown
above. The 'Passive House' framework adds heating and cooling annual energy demand targets on top of that top line
figure. The peak-heating and peak-cooling loads are worth comparing against the same limits. Certification does not
require every one of these limits, but meeting them where possible is good practice.

The limits shown against each result below are the PHI EnerPHit energy-demand limits for this climate. They are the
strictest reference points available for a retrofit of this type, and only the last variant meets the heating target.
That is the expected outcome for a landmarked masonry row house with a large north-facing front elevation. It is also
why our recommendation is calibrated to the peak loads and to the achievable envelope, and not to the demand limit.

The peak heating and peak cooling loads are the numbers the mechanical designer will use. Under the recommended
package the whole-building peak heating load falls to roughly 35,000 Btu/h and peak sensible cooling to roughly
16,000 Btu/h, about 1.3 tons. Both are small enough for a single compact all-electric system. That is the practical
payoff of the envelope work.
