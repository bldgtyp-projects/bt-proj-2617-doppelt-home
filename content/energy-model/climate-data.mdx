The data from this climate set is illustrated here for reference purposes. Passive House energy models use monthly
average climate data, so the plots may look different from the more typical ASHRAE hourly data shown in some other US
energy modeling programs. Both sets come from the same local weather stations.

The site sits in ASHRAE Climate Zone <Var k="narrative.climate.ashrae_location_name" />, a mixed-humid climate with a
winter design temperature of <Var k="narrative.climate.ashrae_winter_design_temp_F" />°F
(<Var k="narrative.climate.ashrae_winter_design_temp_C" />°C). Heating and cooling both matter here, and summer latent
load is a real design constraint. That drives our recommendation for a lower-SHGC glazing package and a ventilation
system with humidity control. It also explains the summer resilience result on the [Resilience](/resilience/) page.
