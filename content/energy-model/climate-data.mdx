The plots below show that data. Passive House energy models use monthly average climate data, so they may look
different from the ASHRAE hourly data shown in some other US energy modeling programs. Both sets come from the same
local weather stations.

The site sits in ASHRAE Climate <Var k="narrative.climate.ashrae_location_name" />, a mixed-humid climate with a
winter design temperature of <Var k="narrative.climate.ashrae_winter_design_temp_F" />°F
(<Var k="narrative.climate.ashrae_winter_design_temp_C" />°C). Heating and cooling both matter here, and summer latent
load is a real design constraint. That drives our recommendation for a lower-SHGC glazing package and a ventilation
system with humidity control. It also explains the summer resilience result on the [Resilience](/resilience/) page.
