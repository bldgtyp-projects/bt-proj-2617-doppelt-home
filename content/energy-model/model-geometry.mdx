All model results shown here use the building geometry below. It follows the schematic drawing set received
<Var k="source_files.cad_files_received_date" /> and describes all seven levels of the proposed building, from the
lowered basement through the new penthouse, including the rear extension at the garden and parlor levels and the
fourth-floor terrace.

The geometry includes both the building and the local shading context. For details on the context shading see the
[windows site shading section](/windows/).

An envelope-to-floor-area ratio of <Var k="narrative.user_defined.envelope_to_icfa_ratio" /> is high for a row house
this size. A tall, narrow building with a full-height stair and a small footprint carries more skin per square foot of
living space than a wide one. That ratio is why envelope quality does more work here than equipment efficiency.

<div class="btwr-callout">
  Existing wall masonry is noted on the drawing set as assumed. Confirm masonry thickness, backup construction, and the bearing condition of the embedded floor joists by probe or demolition survey before the assembly design is fixed.
</div>
