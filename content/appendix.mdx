#### Assembly naming

The variant tables carry the assembly names used in the energy model. They map to the physical assemblies as follows:

| Model name | Physical assembly |
| --- | --- |
| Ext_Wall | Above-grade exterior masonry wall |
| Ground_Wall | Below-grade foundation wall |
| Ground_Floor | Basement slab on grade |
| Ext_Floor | Floor exposed to outdoor air (rear extension soffit, terrace) |
| Ext_Roof | Roof to outdoor air |
| Generic Interior Wall | Party wall to the adjoining building |

#### Open items carried into the next phase

- Existing masonry thickness, backup construction and joist-bearing condition are noted as assumed on the drawing set
  and need verification by probe or demolition survey.
- Hygrothermal (WUFI-Pro) analysis of the front wall, the rear wall and a party wall, including the embedded
  joist-end check, is not yet done. Assembly depths above are performance targets pending that work.
- Thermal-bridge psi-values are carried as a 10% allowance. Two-dimensional simulation of the governing details will
  replace that allowance with calculated values.
- The penthouse-roof PV array shown on the drawing set is not yet in the model.
- Mechanical equipment is modeled as generic all-electric heat-pump systems. Equipment selection, load calculations
  and duct design are by others.
- The window package for the landmarked front elevation is unresolved pending the Landmarks discussion.
- Interior and exterior shading are not yet specified. Both carry more weight in the summer resilience result than
  any other passive measure, and the outage analysis will be revisited once products are selected.
- Ground heat transfer in the outage analysis uses a simplified boundary condition. A two-dimensional ground model is
  carried to a later phase.

#### Units

Energy demand figures are reported as whole-building annual totals in kWh/yr, not per unit of floor area. Where a
Passive House limit is written per unit of area, we have multiplied it by this building's treated floor area of
<Var k="narrative.user_defined.icfa_ft2" /> ft<sup>2</sup> (365.4 m<sup>2</sup>) and reported the resulting
whole-building allowance. Peak loads and equipment sizing stay in Btu/h. For assembly U-values, 1 W/(m<sup>2</sup>K)
is about 0.176 Btu/hr-ft<sup>2</sup>-F.
