TBS confirms the sheet numbers and responsibility split before issue. The November cover sheet anticipates EN-001 through EN-003.

- [ ] **EN-001 · TBS with BLDGTYP and trade RDPs.** Professional statement, selected code/path, energy analysis/COMcheck reference, supporting documentation index, and applicable credit, TR8 and commissioning information.
- [ ] **EN-002 · TBS with BLDGTYP.** Thermal-envelope and air-barrier diagrams, assembly sections and U/C/F values keyed to details, including the addition and lower-level scope.
- [ ] **EN-003 · TBS with BLDGTYP and the window supplier.** Fenestration areas and ratings, replacement-window checks, and thermal-bridge documentation with detail references.
- [ ] **MEP / plumbing / lighting drawings · respective trade RDPs.** Equipment, distributions, insulation, controls and analyses coordinated with the envelope and any credits.
- [ ] **Review backup · BLDGTYP for envelope, trade RDPs for systems.** Methodology, sourced input register, calculation backup, dated compliance reports, and product/thermal-analysis evidence. TBS includes the Historic Building Report and approvals where required.
- [ ] **DOB NOW submission · applicant of record / expediter.** Confirm the required EN Plan file and trade-file arrangement for the actual job type, signatures, attestations and submission receipt.

[ECC 101.5.2 and 103](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/codes-pdf/energy_code_2025/2025nycecc_c1.pdf#page=4) · [DOB supporting-document guidance](https://home4.nyc.gov/site/buildings/codes/nycecc-frequently-asked-questions.page) · [1 RCNY §5000-01](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/rules/1_RCNY_5000-01_2020ECC.pdf)

After submission, the applicant tracks DOB objections and coordinates revisions with the responsible RDP. BLDGTYP updates the envelope analysis when the design changes. During construction, the approved agency records the required inspections and tests. At closeout, the team reconciles the approved and installed design, submits any required as-built analysis, and provides the envelope certificate.
