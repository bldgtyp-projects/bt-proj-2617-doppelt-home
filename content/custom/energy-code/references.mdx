- [C402.1.4 component performance](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/codes-pdf/energy_code_2025/2025nycecc_c4.pdf#page=7), [C402.5 fenestration](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/codes-pdf/energy_code_2025/2025nycecc_c4.pdf#page=13), [C402.6 air leakage](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/codes-pdf/energy_code_2025/2025nycecc_c4.pdf#page=16), and [C402.7 thermal bridges](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/codes-pdf/energy_code_2025/2025nycecc_c4.pdf#page=21).
- [Chapter C5](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/codes-pdf/energy_code_2025/2025nycecc_c5.pdf), including C501.5 historic features, C501.6 conditioning changes, C502 additions, C503 alterations and C505 occupancy/use changes. [AC §28-101.4.5](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/newyorkcity/latest/NYCadmin/0-0-0-155462) governs the retained-floor/extent-of-work screen.
- [1 RCNY §5000-01 posted rule](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/rules/1_RCNY_5000-01_2020ECC.pdf) and [adopted amendments effective December 10](https://rules.cityofnewyork.us/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Final-Rule-NYCECC.pdf).
- [DOB general guide](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/pdf/general25.pdf), [envelope guide](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/pdf/envelope25.pdf), [mechanical guide](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/pdf/mechanical_guide25.pdf), [lighting/power guide](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/pdf/le_power25.pdf), and [other requirements guide](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/pdf/other_req25.pdf).
- [DOB NYCECC FAQs](https://home4.nyc.gov/site/buildings/codes/nycecc-frequently-asked-questions.page), [Historic Building Report guide](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/pdf/historic_report_guide.pdf), and [March 24 historic-building service notice](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/pdf/nycecc_guidance-sn.pdf).

- [DOB NOW step-by-step Energy Submission guide](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/pdf/dob_now_build_energy_submission_step_by_step_guide.pdf) and [DOB NOW portal](https://a810-dobnow.nyc.gov/publish/Index.html#!/). The interface guide dates to 2021; use it with the current NYCECC and DOB FAQ, including the 2025 historic-building requirements.
