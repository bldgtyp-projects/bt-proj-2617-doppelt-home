The dates below work backward from December 4. All six milestone checks are open as of November 24. Complete a check when the listed evidence is on file and the responsible lead has confirmed it.

### 1. Confirm the filing basis

**Lead · TBS / expediter. Target · November 25.**

- [ ] Close F01/F02 with the legal use, signing parties, work boundaries and filing basis recorded. Confirm the addition and cellar treatment, the credit screen, and any LPC/DOB determination required to complete the set.

### 2. Freeze the geometry and opening areas

**Lead · BLDGTYP with TBS / Mechanical. Target · November 30.**

- [ ] Close E01 and reconcile E03 opening IDs to the current plans/elevations. Complete the envelope diagram, wall and roof quantities, window-to-wall and skylight-to-roof ratios, ground-contact areas, slab perimeters and applicable bridge quantities.

### 3. Confirm the assemblies and rated products

**Lead · TBS / Structural / window supplier, with BLDGTYP. Target · November 30.**

- [ ] Close E02/E03/E04 with coordinated sections, supported product ratings and the bridge schedule. BLDGTYP completes the thermal calculations needed for the proposed factors.

### 4. Close the other disciplines and credit decisions

**Lead · TBS coordinates Mechanical, Plumbing and Lighting/Electrical. Target · November 30.**

- [ ] Close M01/M02/L01/P01/I01 with the system analyses, PV/credit basis and inspection/commissioning requirements. Each discipline identifies the signed drawings that support its analysis.

### 5. Verify the envelope and resolve the street windows

**Lead · BLDGTYP with TBS's signing RDP. Target · December 1.**

- [ ] Produce the passing COMcheck report and reconciled Equation 4-1 backup for the agreed scope. Complete SHGC, replacement-window, air-leakage and mandatory checks. Resolve the historic window/report route and record any required DOB disposition.

### 6. Review, seal and submit the coordinated set

**Lead · TBS / applicant of record, with BLDGTYP and trade RDPs. Targets · coordination December 2; signatures December 3; submission December 4.**

- [ ] Complete the RDP review and resolve comments against the keyed drawings and calculations.
- [ ] Assemble the signed EN package and trade analyses, complete the required DOB NOW entries/uploads, and submit the coordinated permit set. Record the submission confirmation and job number.

If an input or determination changes these dates, TBS and BLDGTYP record the revised handoff date and the affected item on this page.
