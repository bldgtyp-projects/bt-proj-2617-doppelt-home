The working route is **2025 NYCECC Commercial Provisions, Climate Zone 4A**, applied through Chapter C5 for the existing building. The building exceeds the energy code's three-story residential limit. TBS will record the legal occupancy and grade/story count in the filing basis. The existing work follows C503, the penthouse addition follows C502, and any newly conditioned lower-level space is checked under C501.6. [C202 definitions, pp. 15 and 16](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/codes-pdf/energy_code_2025/2025nycecc_c2.pdf#page=15) · [C301.1](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/codes-pdf/energy_code_2025/2025nycecc_c3.pdf#page=1) · [Chapter C5](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/codes-pdf/energy_code_2025/2025nycecc_c5.pdf)

1. **Envelope compliance uses the C402.1.4 component performance method in COMcheck.** BLDGTYP enters the applicable assemblies and openings using the **2025 New York City** code selection. The report compares the proposed envelope with the code target. We retain the Equation 4-1 calculation and thermal-bridge backup, and key the values to the drawings. Confirm the calculation boundaries for the addition, altered work and any newly conditioned space with TBS. [C402.1.4](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/codes-pdf/energy_code_2025/2025nycecc_c4.pdf#page=7) · [DOB guidance on COMcheck](https://home4.nyc.gov/site/buildings/codes/nycecc-frequently-asked-questions.page) · [COMcheck-Web](https://comcheck.energycode.pnl.gov/)
2. **The design disciplines document the other requirements.** Mechanical prepares HVAC/ventilation compliance, Plumbing/MEP prepares service water heating, and Lighting/Electrical prepares lighting/power. TBS coordinates a common code path and the required credits, PV, inspections and commissioning statements. Each discipline supplies its analysis and drawing references. [C502.3 and C503](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/codes-pdf/energy_code_2025/2025nycecc_c5.pdf#page=2) · [DOB commercial tabular analysis template](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/excel/2025_NYCECC_Tabular_Analysis_2025c.xlsx)
3. **The NYS registered design professionals review and seal the filing documents.** BLDGTYP prepares the envelope analysis and backup for TBS's adoption. TBS and the trade RDPs assemble the coordinated permit set. The applicant of record submits the required EN documents and related trade drawings in DOB NOW. [ECC 101.5.2](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/codes-pdf/energy_code_2025/2025nycecc_c1.pdf#page=4) · [1 RCNY §5000-01](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/rules/1_RCNY_5000-01_2020ECC.pdf)

COMcheck is the envelope trade-off within this prescriptive compliance route. The work plan relies on each discipline meeting its applicable requirements, with the envelope trade-off documented under C402.1.4. [DOB general guide, GE-11](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/pdf/general25.pdf#page=12)

### Decisions required before the analysis closes

- **Street-window treatment.** Check replacement sash/glazing under C503.2.2.1 and Table C402.5, including category-specific U-factor averaging. TBS confirms LPC direction and the historic-report requirement. Any proposed feature-specific exception needs the required report and DOB approval. [C503.2.2.1](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/codes-pdf/energy_code_2025/2025nycecc_c5.pdf#page=5) · [C501.5](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/codes-pdf/energy_code_2025/2025nycecc_c5.pdf#page=1) · [Historic Building Report guide](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/pdf/historic_report_guide.pdf)
- **Credits and PV.** TBS/expediter records the addition area and substantial-improvement determination. The team checks C502.3.7 and C503.6 for applicable efficiency credits and C502.3.8/C405.15 for the addition's renewable requirement. Record the basis for each exception or the measures being used. [C502.3.7 and C502.3.8](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/codes-pdf/energy_code_2025/2025nycecc_c5.pdf#page=3) · [C503.6](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/codes-pdf/energy_code_2025/2025nycecc_c5.pdf#page=9) · [C405.15](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/codes-pdf/energy_code_2025/2025nycecc_c4.pdf#page=135)
- **Filing rules and job type.** TBS/expediter confirms the job and work-type split, signing parties, and required uploads. The amended §5000-01 rule takes effect December 10, 2026, after the target submission. Confirm the rules and DOB NOW requirements applicable at actual filing. [DOB code and forms](https://www.nyc.gov/site/buildings/codes/energy-conservation-code.page) · [Adopted rule and effective date](https://rules.cityofnewyork.us/rule/amendments-related-to-the-nyc-energy-conservation-code/)
