Size the fresh-air system around the room-by-room supply, transfer and extract strategy, not around a single
whole-building ventilation rate. The table below keeps the modeled airflow assumptions visible for design
coordination and commissioning.

Design airflow for the whole house is approximately <Var k="narrative.user_defined.design_airflow_cfm" /> cfm at the
maximum setting and roughly <Var k="narrative.user_defined.standard_airflow_cfm" /> cfm in normal operation. Supply is allocated to the bedrooms, the den, the playroom,
the office, the sitting room and the dining room. Extract is drawn from the bathrooms, the kitchen, the pantry, the
laundry, the mudroom and the powder rooms. The stair halls act as the transfer path, which suits a row house where
the stair already connects every level.

The kitchen extract hood is modeled separately and is not part of the balanced airflow. See
[Appliances and Venting](#appliances-and-venting) for how it interacts with a tight envelope.

The plans on this page mark the intended supply, transfer and extract locations for coordination with the mechanical
designer. They are a design-intent layout, not a duct-routing drawing.
