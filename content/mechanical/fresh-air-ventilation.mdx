An airtight, low-energy building in this climate needs dedicated mechanical ventilation with heat recovery to deliver
enough fresh air for good indoor air quality (IAQ) in all seasons.

Do not over-ventilate in winter. Indoor air gets too dry, with health and comfort consequences. To keep airflow rates
down while holding indoor air quality, we recommend a **Supply-Transfer-Extract** configuration rather than supplying
and extracting from each individual room.

Supply air should go to bedrooms and living spaces. Extract air should be drawn from bathrooms, the kitchen, and any
storage spaces. Provide transfer openings between supply and extract spaces, such as door undercuts or transfer
grilles.

This configuration cuts duct-borne sound transmission between spaces, mixes fresh air better, and needs smaller and
cheaper ducting.

<div class="btwr-callout btwr-callout--critical">
  <strong>Coordination note</strong>
  <span>
    Install the fresh-air ventilation system so it is completely independent of the heating and cooling system. There
    is no effective way to combine fresh-air ducting with heating/cooling ducting, so allocate space for both.
  </span>
</div>
